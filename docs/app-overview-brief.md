# 🚗 Office Car Booking — App Overview

> Internal web app that replaces manual Excel-based car booking and eliminates
> double-booking. One deployed web app — no installs.

---

## 👤 User (Employee) Perspective

Book a company car in seconds — pick a **vehicle → day → time slot**, add trip
details, and submit. Slots lock instantly to prevent double-booking. View,
track, and cancel your own bookings, receive email notifications, set your
password via OTP, and submit in-app feedback — all from a mobile-friendly
interface.

---

## 🛠️ Office Admin Perspective

Manage the full fleet and all bookings from a desktop console — **approve or
reject** requests, add/edit vehicles, and promote other admins. Monitor live
dashboards (utilisation, trends, peak hours), export reports to Excel, review
feedback, and enforce rules — weekly limits, no-show strikes, and auto-blocking
— all configurable in settings.

---

### ✨ Why it matters

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Manual Excel sheet | Self-service web app |
| Frequent double-bookings | Slots lock on submit — impossible to double-book |
| No visibility | Live dashboards + Excel exports |
| Ad-hoc rules | Configurable limits & auto-enforcement |
