## 🔧 Implementation details

### Backend (`dashboardController` + `routes/dashboard.js`, `requireRole('admin')`)
- **summary:** `total_vehicles` = active vehicles; `todays_bookings` = bookings where booking_date = today; `utilisation_rate` = booked/total slots for today (or current week — pick + document); `most_used_vehicle` = vehicle with most bookings (range or all-time).
- **utilisation:** per active vehicle, `used_slots` = count(approved/completed) in [from,to], `total_slots` = days × 5, `pct`.
- **booking-trend:** `GROUP BY` day/week/month over [from,to]; return `[{period, count}]`.
- **peak-hours:** `GROUP BY slot_start` → `[{slot, count}]`.
- Exclude cancelled/rejected/no_show from "used" counts (document the choice).

### Frontend (`pages/AdminDashboard.jsx`)
- Reuse `KPICard` for the 4 cards (grid; responsive).
- Utilisation: simple `Progress` bars per vehicle (no chart lib needed) or a bar chart.
- Trend + peak: chosen chart lib. Keep charts lazy-imported to limit bundle growth.
- Date range control (default last 30 days) feeding utilisation/trend.
